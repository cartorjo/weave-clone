What we deliver

We deliver results and stand behind them.

With the usual market models, risk and control stay with the client. We take full responsibility for a defined service. From start to finish.

01

We optimize.

We take over the tasks that have to run so that your business runs, and hand them back improved. We automate where it pays off, measured transparently against jointly defined KPIs. We provide ongoing services with agreed service levels, response times and escalation paths. Your team can focus on your core business.

More stable. Faster. Traceable.

02

We transform.

We rebuild your processes and your value creation with innovative technologies, calculated together before you invest. We deliver into your production environment and fine-tune. Where regulation requires it, we test together against defined criteria and a qualification and validation plan.

Making technology productive.

03

We scale.

Behind our in-house team of over 250 professionals stands the Hays network, with another 3,000 specialists and 10,000 active partner companies. If your project needs more capacity, our team scales with it. You pay for results and agreed service levels, with no idle costs. Whatever the model or the scale, Emposo remains responsible and your only contractual partner.

Taking impact to scale.

04

We integrate.

With us, industry knowledge, AI and technology expertise work hand in hand. We master the technology, and our experts come straight from your industry. We integrate what works separately in your organization: the business unit and IT. That is what sets us apart: a seamless interface with responsibility for the overall result.

Business value in operations.

Quality and compliance

Quality, security and reliable evidence.

Established methods, quality gates and field-tested compliance make risks manageable. Our quality management is certified to ISO 9001, our compliance management system to ISO 37301. We share the result of our TISAX assessment via the ENX portal; scopes and evidence are available on request.

Methods for delivery
